Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Don't Drop It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

Your progress (stars and best scores), coins, upgrades and settings (sound and haptics) are stored only on your device.

The game has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not use any third-party SDKs.

In-app purchases (coin packs) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Game Center: if you choose to sign in, your Endless score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. Apple handles that data under Apple's own privacy policy.

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Questions? Contact us here: https://github.com/SeungbinBaik/dont-drop-it-support/issues

隱私權政策

最後更新： 2026 年 9 月 27 日

Don't Drop It 不會蒐集、販售或分享任何個人資料。

你的進度（星星與最佳分數）、金幣、升級與設定（音效與震動）只會儲存在你的裝置上。

遊戲沒有廣告、沒有分析、沒有追蹤，也不使用任何第三方 SDK。

App 內購買（金幣包）由 Apple 處理，我們不會收到你的付款資訊。

Game Center：若你選擇登入，無盡模式的分數會傳送到 Apple 的 Game Center 排行榜，並依 Apple 的隱私權政策處理。

本遊戲適合所有年齡，也不會刻意蒐集兒童的資訊。
